# Who Could Become Richer Than Elon Musk?

(0:00) Elon Musk is so wealthy that the second-richest person in the world is hundreds of billions of dollars behind him.
(0:08) But what if someone actually catches up?
(0:11) There are a few billionaires with businesses powerful enough to challenge his position. And some of them might surprise you.
(0:19) Let’s look at the most interesting possibilities.
(0:23) First, there’s Jeff Bezos, the founder of Amazon.
(0:27) Bezos built one of the most powerful companies in modern history. Amazon started as an online bookstore and eventually became a giant in e-commerce, cloud computing, logistics and entertainment.
(0:39) Its cloud business, Amazon Web Services, is especially important because companies around the world depend on it to run their digital operations.
(0:49) If Amazon continues growing and its cloud business becomes even more valuable, Bezos could see his fortune increase dramatically.
(0:57) But catching Musk would still require an extraordinary rise.
(1:03) Then there’s Google’s co-founders, Larry Page and Sergey Brin.
(1:08) These two helped build Google into one of the most influential companies on Earth.
(1:14) Google controls a massive digital advertising business, owns YouTube, operates Android and has a major presence in artificial intelligence through Google DeepMind.
(1:25) And here’s what makes them interesting: both still own substantial stakes in Alphabet, Google’s parent company.
(1:33) If Google’s AI investments translate into enormous profits and its stock continues rising, their personal fortunes could grow significantly.
(1:43) In fact, Page is currently among the people closest to Musk on the billionaire rankings.
(1:51) Another serious contender is Mark Zuckerberg.
(1:55) He built Facebook, but his business empire now includes Instagram, WhatsApp and other major platforms through Meta.
(2:04) Billions of people use Meta’s products, giving the company an enormous audience to monetise.
(2:12) Zuckerberg is also investing heavily in artificial intelligence and smart glasses.
(2:18) If Meta successfully turns those investments into major new businesses, his wealth could climb much higher.
(2:29) Here’s the problem.
(2:31) According to Forbes’ real-time billionaire estimates on November 26, 2026, Musk’s fortune stands at roughly $823 billion.
(2:40) Jeff Bezos is around $365 billion, while Larry Page is around $227 billion.
(2:48) That’s not a small difference. Even the closest contenders would need extraordinary growth to overtake him.
(2:56) And Musk’s fortune is heavily tied to the value of companies like Tesla and SpaceX. A major change in either company’s valuation could move his wealth dramatically.
(3:06) So, who is most likely to become richer than him?
(3:10) Larry Page, Sergey Brin, Jeff Bezos and Mark Zuckerberg are among the names worth watching.
(3:17) But nobody has a guaranteed path to the top.
(3:21) Because in the world of billionaires, the person in second place isn’t necessarily the person who will finish first.
(3:28) And Elon Musk’s biggest competitor might not even be obvious yet.
